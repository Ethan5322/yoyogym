Common questions

I forgot my membership number

It is on your membership card and in the confirmation your gym sent when you joined. Your gym’s reception can also look it up for you.

I don’t remember which gym I joined

Tap I’m a member, then Find my gym, then I don’t remember which gym I joined. Your membership number and phone number will tell us.

My gym isn’t on Yoyo Gyms

Not every gym has joined yet. Ask your gym to apply — owners start from I’m a gym owner on the first screen.

How do I cancel my membership?

Your membership is with your gym, so your gym handles cancellations and payments. Speak to reception, or contact your gym directly.
